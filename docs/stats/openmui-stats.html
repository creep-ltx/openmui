Against Zune68 Beta 5

Zune68 Beta 5 (2026.11.22, as released) and OpenMUI after the speed round of 2026-11-23, the same test on the same machine (A1200, OS 3.1), measured in one session on 2026-11-23. Each bar is the library's time as a share of MUI 3.8's; shorter is faster.

On OS 3.2.1 (Zune68 reports MUI 5 on every OS) Zune68 is faster than MUI 5 in 24 of 27 operations; cycle set, radio set and list insert of one row take 1.5 to 1.8 times MUI 5's time. OpenMUI is faster than MUI 5 in all 27, and Zune68 takes 1.02 to 3.78 times OpenMUI's time in 26 of them; menu open + close is level (20.0 ms, both ask for IDCMP_MENUVERIFY there).
